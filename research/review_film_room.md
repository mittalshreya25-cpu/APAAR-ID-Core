## Specific challenge to the baseline demo

The transcript's worked “case D” vacant-seat warning must not be reused as a correct current 2026-27 result. It can remain a **historical/provisional policy fixture** labelled with its rule source/version, or become an amendment-conflict demonstration returning applicability-review-required. A warning outcome alone would disguise unresolved eligibility. Handbook validity dates are insufficient to dismiss a later modification. Conversely, indexed amendment excerpts and a peer's reading cannot certify complete current law.

For the eventual rule catalogue, attach year, programme, level, course, approval evidence and policy-version provenance to each evaluation. Unknown applicability should create an explainable review case. Do not infer eligibility merely from an NRI/OCI/CIWG boolean or copy maxima into every institution's capacity.
