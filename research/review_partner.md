## Challenges resolved and remaining

1. **Identity versus format:** attempted falsification by checking issuance/status language rather than trusting the “12-digit” description alone. The original page distinguishes creation/authentication from the number's shape. PARTNER-1 survives with its inference label and limitation.
2. **Consent overreach:** inspected both the ordinary consent statements and the narrower enquiry/investigation exception. PARTNER-3 survives as a platform-policy claim. It must not become a general privacy-law conclusion.
3. **Official API access overreach:** NAD evidence does not itself prove ABC onboarding or an endpoint contract. The independently opened APISetu Overview supports general publisher approval, but the collection shell does not certify particular API availability. The original PARTNER-4 compound scope was marked partial and a focused objection sent to the chair. See the non-author resolution below; endpoint/catalog limitations remain.
4. **Outdated handbook arithmetic:** read Partner's currentness limitation against `research/film_room.md`. Film Room reports an indexed government-hosted 2026–27 modified clause and a conflicting treatment of unfilled international seats, but labels PDF retrieval/amendment-chain verification incomplete. I did not independently retrieve that addendum in this review and do not certify its detailed caps. This reinforces keeping PARTNER-6 historical, rather than refuting its documented base distinction.

No claim was contradicted within its stated scope. The important unresolved issue is full current capacity-rule applicability, not whether the baseline text contains the quoted within-intake distinction. The prototype should use clearly labelled reviewed demo rules until the amendment and institutional approval evidence is complete.
